# **Call of Dutie: Snack Ops (SDK)**

**A fan-made, multiversal first-person shooter framework developed by Iron Will Interactive With Help From Kelint.** Heavily inspired by *Black Ops 1*, *Call of Duty 2 & 3*, *NX1 Future Warfare*, *Modern Warfare 3 (2011)*, and *Black Ops 2*, this non-profit project is built from the ground up as a love letter to the golden era of the franchise, combining iconic gameplay, maps, and mechanics with completely fresh systems.

## 📖 **Overview**

*Call of Dutie: Snack Ops* is an independent, non-profit tribute to the *Call of Duty* series. We are building a robust SDK framework in Unreal Engine 5 that captures the classic feel while implementing modern procedural systems, true first-person perspectives, and modular blueprint designs. About 90% of the project's baseline logic and original assets are currently owned and developed by our team, subject to change across future forks and updates.

## ✨ **Newly Added Features**

* **Movement Overhaul & Dolphin Diving:** Completely proceduralized movement systems featuring True First-Person perspective, Tactical Sprinting, and the newly implemented Dolphin Dive (press crouch while sprinting to dive with mid-air aiming).
* **Controller Support:** Full controller support is now implemented for Windows controllers or any gamepad with proper PC drivers.
* **Ladder Climbing:** Fully functional ladder climbing logic has been implemented directly into the player character, accompanied by an easy-to-use drag-and-drop Blueprint.
* **Drag-and-Drop Editor Blueprints:** Allows new artists or users to effortlessly place fully functioning items, doors, pickups, traps, and much more into the world in a matter of seconds.
* **Bot AI:** Introduced configurable difficulty settings for different enemy bots.

## 🎮 **Core Features & Game Modes**

* **Zombies Mode:** Fight through endless waves of zombies. Future updates will introduce **portals** for enhanced, multiversal gameplay.
* **Team Deathmatch (TDM):** Play offline with highly configurable bots. **LAN support** is coming soon, with full **online multiplayer** in active development.
* **Classic Gunplay:** Classic movement inspired by *Call of Duty 2*, now updated with modern mechanics. A dedicated **knife mechanic** replaces the traditional weapon stock bash.

## 🗺️ **Assets, Weapons & Maps**

* **Maps:** A curated mix of custom-made arenas and ported classics like **Nuketown OG**, **Rust**, and **Underground**. Larger, expansive maps are currently in development.
* **Guns & Loadouts:** A massive selection of weaponry. Gun mods, custom camos, and full loadout systems are currently in the planning phase.
* **Original & Ported Assets:** Most player equipment and textures are completely original, built from scratch, and reworked for a fresh UE5 look. Select iconic maps, guns, player models, and sounds are ported from legendary titles and completely reworked to fit the modern engine.
* **Custom Sound Design:** The game will feature **custom, never-before-heard voice lines** from fan-favorite voice actors to deliver a completely unique audio experience.

## 🚀 **Roadmap & Planned Features**

* **Survival & Spec Ops Modes:** Cooperative modes offering highly replayable, objective-based experiences.
* **Expanded Arsenal:** More weapons and brand-new weapon types will be rolling out shortly.
* **Vehicles:** We are currently deep in R&D to implement properly functioning vehicles based on *Call of Duty 3* mechanics. *(Please wait for our first vehicle release before inquiring about ETA!)*
* **Full Overhaul:** We plan to eventually overhaul all current placeholder assets with 100% new meshes, refined animations, and modern aesthetics before forking the project into an original IP.

## 🛠️ **UGC & Modding**

* **Weapon & Vehicle Mods:** In the future, developers, modders, and players will be able to create new guns and vehicles, add custom sounds, and tweak values like fire rate, damage, and handling.
* **User Generated Content:** Full community mod support for gameplay and custom maps is planned for later down the line. *Note: We do not actively support modding just yet, as it will require an extensive reviewal process and stable SDK build first.*

## 💻 **Download Notes & Requirements**

> **Note:** Non-editor builds are not yet available. You will need the editor to play and test this SDK.

* **Engine:** Unreal Engine 5.3.2 (Must be installed on your C:\ Drive to use the SDK or Play in Editor).
* **OS:** Windows 11 or higher is strictly required to run this program/editor.
